Self-service

Signed-in users can export all their data (Settings → Privacy → Export) and delete their account (Settings → Privacy → Delete account) at any time.

Requests

Use the privacy request form for access, correction, export or deletion. We may need to verify your identity. We aim to respond within [RESPONSE PERIOD set by counsel, e.g. 30 days].

What deletion does

  • Sign-in stops and every session ends immediately.
  • LaunchPad revokes its Roblox tokens and Studio pairings and cancels active subscriptions.
  • Your account, games, reports, scans, connections and preferences are deleted.
  • Billing references (Paddle IDs, amounts, dates) are kept without your account link for [PERIOD] where tax or accounting law requires.
  • Security logs are kept for up to [365] days and then deleted.
  • Encrypted backups roll off within [BACKUP RETENTION, e.g. 30 days]. Deleted data isn't restored from backups except to recover from an incident, and is then deleted again.

Retention periods (configurable)

  • Older scan summaries: [90] days (the latest per experience is kept while the account exists).
  • Security events: [365] days.
  • Sent email copies: 30 days, with one-time links removed after sending.
  • Finished background jobs: 30 days.
