LaunchPad uses only strictly necessary cookies. We don't use advertising or analytics cookies.

  • Session cookie (lp_session / __Host-lp_session): keeps you signed in. HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax. Expires after [30] days or [7] days of inactivity.
  • Google sign-in nonce (lp_gnonce): protects a Google sign-in attempt. Lasts 10 minutes.
  • Roblox connection state (lp_rbx): protects a Roblox authorization attempt. Lasts 10 minutes.

Google and Paddle may set their own cookies when you use Google sign-in or Paddle checkout; see their policies. Browser storage is used for display preferences and the demo, which never leaves your browser.
