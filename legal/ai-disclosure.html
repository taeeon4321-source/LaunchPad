LaunchPad's five optimizers produce scores and recommendations automatically.

  • How it works today: transparent, rule-based analysis and calculations that run on LaunchPad. No machine-learning model or external AI provider is used, and no image-recognition service inspects your artwork. Thumbnail feedback is based on file checks and your own answers.
  • Evidence: every scanned finding shows where it came from (your Studio scan, Roblox, your answers, a calculation or an estimate). Things LaunchPad can't see are marked as insufficient data, not guessed.
  • Limits: scores are LaunchPad's own assessment. They are not an official Roblox ranking and don't predict Roblox's recommendations or guarantee results.
  • Training: your private source code and game data aren't used to train general-purpose AI models.
  • Future changes: if an external AI provider is added, we'll name it, state exactly what is sent and how long the provider keeps it (as supported by the actual contract), and ask for consent where required.
