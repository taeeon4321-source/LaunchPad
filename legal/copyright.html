Creators keep ownership of their games, scripts and artwork. LaunchPad processes them only to provide its service.

Reporting a concern

If you believe content on LaunchPad infringes your rights, use the copyright report form and include: your contact details; the affected content and its URL or asset identifier; an explanation of the rights you hold; a good-faith statement that the use isn't authorized; and any supporting information.

What we do

Every report is logged, reviewed by a person and answered. We may remove or restrict content while we review and contact the person who provided it.
