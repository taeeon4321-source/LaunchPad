Annual plans are charged in full, once, at the start of each yearly period. Savings are calculated against twelve monthly payments of the same plan.

Merchant of record and taxes

Payments are processed by Paddle, which acts as merchant of record. Applicable taxes are calculated and shown by Paddle at checkout and may be added to or included in the price depending on your location. Prices shown in another currency are conversions by Paddle and may differ slightly.

Automatic renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period (monthly or yearly) at the then-current price until you cancel. We'll notify you before any price change.

Cancelling

Cancel any time in Settings → Billing (Paddle's customer portal). Cancellation takes effect at the end of the period you've paid for; you keep paid features until then.

Plan changes

Upgrades and downgrades are prorated by Paddle. After a downgrade, your reports and games are kept; features and limits beyond the new plan stop applying and you choose which connected experiences stay active.

Failed payments

If a renewal payment fails, Paddle retries it and we'll email you. Paid features stop as soon as a payment fails and resume when it's paid; there is no grace period. Your data is never deleted because of a failed payment.
