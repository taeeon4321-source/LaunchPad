Cancellation

You can cancel at any time in Settings → Billing. Monthly and annual plans stay active until the end of the period already paid for and then don't renew. Cancelling stops future charges; it doesn't refund the current period.

No refunds

Payments are non-refundable, including for partially used periods, unused features and annual plans, except where the law requires a refund. This policy doesn't limit any right you have under mandatory consumer law.

Where the law gives you a right to a refund

  • Right of withdrawal (EU, EEA, UK and similar): consumers can normally withdraw from a digital-service contract within 14 days. At checkout we ask you to request that your subscription starts immediately and to acknowledge that you lose the right of withdrawal once the service has started (or, where the law says so, that a proportionate amount may be charged for the days used).   • Faulty service: if LaunchPad doesn't work as described and we can't fix it, you may be entitled to a repair, price reduction or refund under local law.
  • Billing errors: duplicate or mistaken charges are refunded in full.

Paddle

Paddle is the merchant of record and processes any refund the law or Paddle's own buyer terms require, to the original payment method. Paddle's buyer terms also apply to your purchase.

How to ask

Use the refund request form. We record every request and decision.
