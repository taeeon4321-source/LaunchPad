Connecting Roblox

Connecting uses Roblox's official OAuth 2.0 authorization. You choose which experiences to authorize and can revoke access at any time in LaunchPad (Settings → Connected accounts) or in your Roblox account settings. LaunchPad never asks for your Roblox password or browser cookies.

The LaunchPad Studio plugin

  • Read-only: it never edits scripts, deletes objects, changes settings, publishes, uploads assets, changes products or touches player data.
  • It pairs with your account using a short-lived single-use code, then a revocable token kept only in your Studio plugin settings.
  • It only sends scans for experiences Roblox has authorized for your account.
  • Reading scripts is a separate permission you can turn on or off. When on, scripts are read inside Studio and only feature names, script paths and numbers from configuration tables are sent; raw source code is not. Scanned content is treated as data and never executed.

Data

See the Privacy Policy. Disconnecting Roblox revokes LaunchPad's tokens and pairs; your existing reports stay until you delete them.
