# Legal review checklist

All documents in `legal/` are **drafts, awaiting legal review**. To publish a reviewed version, change its header status to `approved`, set the effective date and bump the version; the site shows version, effective date, last update and status.

| Document | File | Open questions for counsel |
|---|---|---|
| Terms of Service | `legal/terms.html` | Operator identity and address; minimum age and parental consent; liability/warranty; governing law; dispute resolution |
| Privacy Policy | `legal/privacy.html` | Controller details; legal bases; subprocessor list and transfers; regional rights (GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA, others); children's data rules (COPPA etc.) |
| Cookie Policy | `legal/cookies.html` | Whether Google/Paddle frames need consent banners |
| Acceptable Use | `legal/acceptable-use.html` | — |
| Copyright & IP | `legal/copyright.html` | DMCA agent / DSA notice handling; counter-notices; repeat-infringer policy |
| Roblox Integration Terms | `legal/roblox-integration.html` | Compliance with Roblox ToS, OAuth app and plugin policies |
| AI & Automated Analysis | `legal/ai-disclosure.html` | Update if an AI provider is added |
| Data Request & Deletion | `legal/data-requests.html` | Response deadlines; retention periods; backup window |
| Subscription Terms | `legal/subscriptions.html` | Auto-renewal disclosures and reminders; price-change notice; consumer cancellation rights |
| Refund & Cancellation | `legal/refunds.html` | **The refund policy itself is a business decision still to be made** |

## Business items (not legal drafting)
- Confirm the legal business owner, entity and jurisdiction; Paddle onboarding must use the real entity.
- Merchant of record ≠ no tax obligations: confirm income tax and accounting with an accountant.
- **Sample game artwork** (Steal An Egg, Throw Food For Animals, Fish It!) belongs to their creators. Get written permission or replace with original artwork before commercial launch.
- Decide the minimum account age with counsel; the sign-up only asks for confirmation, not date of birth.
